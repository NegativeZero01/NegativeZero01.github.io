## Contents
Apps in `/ipas/me` are my own personal projects.  
Apps in `/ipas/archive` are free older IPAs, IPAs lost due to repositories or hosting sites going down, or ones generally difficult to find.


## The Archive
The purpose of `archive` is strictly to *archive* these older IPAs so that they're easily accessible.  
They are hosted *purely for historical preservation* and are free software.  
I do not claim ownership over any apps in `archive`.

## Takedowns And Information
If you are the original developer of an archived app and wish for it to be removed, please contact me or open an issue, and I will remove it from the repository as soon as possible.
